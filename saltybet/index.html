      How Salty Brawl works

      AI fighters brawl non-stop. Nobody controls them. Before every match, betting is open for a short time: pick Red or Blue, type in a wager and hit the button. You get one bet per match.

      Payouts are parimutuel. When bets lock, both pots are revealed. If your side wins you get your wager back plus your share of the losing pot:
      winnings = wager × (losing pot ÷ winning pot)
      Betting on the underdog pays a lot more. That's where the salt comes from.

      Bailouts. You start with $400. If you go broke you get bailed out to $100 at the next match. Higher ranks get a bigger bailout.

      Game modes. 100 matchmaking matches (fighters of the same tier), then a 16-character tournament, then 25 exhibition matches (anyone vs anyone), then it starts over.

      Tournaments use a separate tournament balance ($1,000 + your rank bonus). You can't drop below it, so going all in costs nothing. When the tournament ends, everything you won on top of it goes into your real balance.

      Tiers: X broken, S, A, B, P potato. If a fighter wins 8 of its last 10 matchmaking matches it moves up a tier. If it loses 8 of 10 it moves down.

      Ranks go up with the number of matches you've bet on (25, 50, 75, 100, 150, …). Each rank raises your bailout and tournament balance.

      Stats: the cards under the stream show every fighter's record, win streak, Elo and base stats. Use them.

      Solo mode runs entirely in your browser and saves your progress. When the site is started with npm start, it runs in online mode instead: everyone connected watches the same fights, bets into the same pots and shares the chat.
